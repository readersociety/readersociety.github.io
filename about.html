Our Story - In a Timeline

Sept. 08, 2025 – Our Genesis

It all started with an idea in Noa's head during Fairview Homeschooler's Panlarong Pinoy event at Britanny Square. She approched Yumi, Isaac and Qorein with the idea and they all wanted to join.

Sept. 09, 2025 – Blooming

The first serious discussion about the book club was held via a chaotic Messenger call among Noa, Yumi, Isaac, and Iris. During the animated discussion of logistics and structure, a solemn, yet playful decision was made: Noa was unanimously bestowed the title of "CEO of the Book Club," officially establishing the group's leadership and process.

Sept. 17, 2025 – Innovation

The prototype of our first webpage, the Fairview Homeschoolers Book Club, has made its debut. Be warned, though: the site's design is an assault on the senses, a triumph of terrible design so visually jarring that it's a genuine miracle if you're not temporarily blind after browsing through it. Proceed at your own risk!!

Sept. 25, 2025 – Innovation

Isaac creates the Band of Bookworms website - AKA version 2 of the book club webpage. It had a clean, modern design but was plagued with invisible errors that only a coder can see.

Oct. 02, 2025 – Expansion

That day marked a defining moment for our group as we hosted our very first official "meet" online using Google Meet. Our initial shyness evaporated (mostly), giving way to lively discussions where we learned a surprising amount about each other—primarily our favorite books.

Oct. 09, 2025 – First FTF Meet!

Our first in-person meeting was a memorable event held at Noa's house, a day that truly defined our dynamic. We had an absolute blast playing all sorts of games, so much so that out of a five-hour gathering, we spent literally only five minutes actually discussing book club matters! Isaac proved to be a great host for our game of Mafia, and amidst the laughter and fun, we solidified our identity and officially named ourselves the "Reader Society."

Oct. 21, 2025 – New website, (yet again)

Isaac decides to make a new website to replace Version 2 of the book club website, yet again lol. Unfortunately you can't visit it, as it was deleted.
