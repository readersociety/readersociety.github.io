Members

Every story is made better by the weird and wonderful people who read it. You all bring a unique flavor to our collective tale, and we wouldn't have it any other way. Thanks for showing up and making our story a little less boring.

CEO Elischa (Noa)

Noa is fifteen years old. She is the second born of four kids, and has an older sister and two youger brothers. In her free time, she enjoys listening to music, reading, and occasianally learning new kpop dances. SHe often spends more time than she should looking for new books, even though her reading list is far from finished. her favourite genres are realistic fiction and fantasy. She will read a range of things though, from novels to manga to reddit posts about the nichest things and video game walkthroughs (even 39 clues, I'm just sensitive).
