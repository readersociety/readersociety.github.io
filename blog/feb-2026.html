Reader Society's Third Meet

Written by Austin (Edited by Isaac)

Jan 21, 2026 · 6 min read

"I wasted time, and now doth time waste me." —William Shakespeare’s Richard II (Act V - Scene 5)

I found that usually when people quote this, it’s about a deep meditation on regret. Yet, as it turns out, it can also apply to our book club’s case—specifically, it was going into a third meetup planning a quick icebreaker. A few hours of Taboo & The Chameleon later, it was the realization at precisely 5:42 PM that we hadn’t even begun our discussion yet.

Starting from the beginning, of course, out of nine members, only two were able to come on time. And it was... awkward, to say the least. Awkward as in the very feeling you get in a waiting room when it feels as though the ceiling suddenly feels a lot more interesting to look at. However, small conversations did end up happening before finally—everyone finally started trickling in at around 4:20 pm. As the others came in, snacks were being set on the table, along with the drinks and the board games. Conversations and laughter followed right after, while the adults talked outside and the kids were playing in the nearby living room.

After the very quick icebreaker, we finally had our discussion about the book assigned by Claudia— The City of Ember. We recapped about what happened in the book, then we talked about what we thought about it, what rating we gave the overall story, and which characters were our favorite and least favorite. And for what it’s worth, it was pretty obvious who everyone’s least favorite character was. (Spoiler alert, definately the Mayor uh huh)

With that out of the way, it was time to announce the book the next host, Yumi, would choose—along with the few rules and guidelines that came with it, which were explained by Noa’s mom. Fortunately, there were no objections, or “nays” by anyone, and everyone seemed to be on the same page. And alas, the next book was finally announced; The first book of the series, Enola Holmes: The Case of the Missing Marquess, by American author Nancy Springer. The book was chosen party to honor International Women's month, which was next month from the meet. (As soon as this was brought to topic, almost everyone subconciously looked at us, the only two guys)

We then wrapped things up with a few games of Mafia, accompanied by toasted siopaos and mac & cheese—and some small talk about when our official meeting day would be. By the end, hours had already flown by without us noticing. I was the one to go home early through an Angkas ride, followed by Qorein (through Tito Jeff's van, accompanied with almost all of the club [excepting Yumi, Isaac and Jamie] for what they called as "support" Side note: Please don't be insulted hehe This is Lolo speaking). The meeting stretched til roughly around 10pm. By that time, us teens' eyes were heavy )a stark contrast to the kids, you were playing wtih wild abandon) and that was the time we went gome, satisfied, eager for the next meet.
