Noa's House Yumi's House Book Club Meet Written by Isaac

Reader Society's Fourth Meet!

Written by Isaac

April 02, 2026 · 6 min read

Two weeks ago Thursday, our reading group gathered at our friend Noa’s cozy home for a night that was equal parts laughter, games, and pure bookish joy. The fun actually started before we even got there—Noa and her dad generously carpooled me, Iris, and Qorein over, setting the tone for an evening of friendship, chatter, and a little friendly chaos.

However, the ride to the meetup was bittersweet. Enroute we found out that two of our members, Austin and Claudia, won't be able to come. When we arrived gone were the usual silentness of our CEO's home. It was just... more silent. Noa, Q, Yumi, Iris and myself hanged out in Yumi's bahay kubo while we waited for the two other member's, Jamie and Ava.

The wait was... unique to say the least. Most of it involved eating Yumi's snack stash, and greeting everyone who passed by (even the tricycles!) with, "Austin?", or "Claudia?" or, "Jamie?" or "Ava?" (Eh you get the point.) Needless to say, we were about to finish Yumi's stash when Noa suggested walking around the subdivision while we wait for Jamie and Ava.

Wouldn't you know it, as soon as we stepped out of the gate who came by inside a sleek, modern Grab but Ava! So down we went, walking around Teresa Heights. So she joined us in our quest to be hungry (joke). And it worked. And as we went back, who should we see but Jamie! The club is complete.

Moving on to the Merting Proper, Yumi, our host of the month, kicked things off with a scavenger hunt that had everyone running, strategizing, and laughing like kids again. Two teams went head-to-head, racing around the house to solve clever clues, all for the coveted prize: iced coffee (we didn't know it then, but uf we did know the prize we would have fought harder XD). There were moments of triumph, moments of “Wait, where’s the next clue?!” and a lot of hilarious missteps along the way, as well as some Desperate Times Call of Desperate Measures events (I won't elaborate hehe). Just know that I actually saw the ending map, but I ignored it as artwork :cry:

After the hunt, things winded down to discussion of the past months book, which was Enola Holmes. We were given little slips of paper to answer the questions and to share it with the club. Then came the artistic challenge: drawing each other on pieces of paper. Some of us revealed secret talents, while others… well, let’s say “unique” interpretations had everyone in stitches. (I'm totally not one of those ppl, uh huh...)

It was my turn as host, and I chose Brandon Sanderson’s Skyward (Book 1) for our featured book of the month. And of course, no night would be complete without the board games! We wrapped up the evening with Cluedo, where yours truly roughed and fumbled when I was explaining the rules. But we surived and Iris won the game. When the night finally came to an end, Noa and her dad kindly carpooled me, Iris, and Qorein back home accompanied with Jamie, giving us a chance to chat and laugh about the night one last time before saying goodbye. Even though Austin and Claudia couldn’t make it, the night was unforgettable. Between scavenger hunts, iced coffee victories, abstract art, and heated Cluedo showdowns, we left with smiling faces, sore cheeks from laughing, and hearts full of excitement for our next gathering. Nights like these remind us why we love this book club: it’s not just about reading stories—it’s about living them together.
