Reader Society: Origins

By Claudia
Jan 09, 2026

On a rainy Thursday afternoon, the book club had decided to meet up and have delightful conversations about a variety of topics while playing games. As we sat around the table, the room was quiet except for the sound of Laufey playing softly in the background. There was a sense of awkwardness in the air, mingling with the aroma of brownies being baked. There was a slight feeling of shyness amongst us. The younger kids, however, did not feel any sense of embarrassment and were causing a ruckus in the living room.

The tense and awkward mood was then replaced with the sounds of laughter and enjoyment as we played some games, and the mood shifted into something light and fun. We played Taboo at first, a game of wittiness and good vocabulary. Then we played Mafia, a thrilling, exciting game where we try to guess who the “killer” is. (With vivid, bloody descriptions by me.) Once the air was light and comfortable, we went to discuss names for our book club. After multiple suggestions and debate, the club’s official name was officially called “Reader Society.”

While having simple conversations amongst each other, Noa, our club leader, came into the room with a stack of books, which were going to be what we’re going to read for the month. The choice was a series of graphic novels known as “The Berrybrook Series” and a popular classic among Studio Ghibli fans, “Howl's Moving Castle.” ​

After a while a bombshell was dropped: the book club actually had a website! Immediately, all of us crowded around the monitor were Isaac showed his created Reader Society website - sadly he said it wasn't finished and was in its prototype stage. He did, however, state that it was almost ready for launching and would be released soon.

As the meeting slowly approached an end, the day and moment were captured in a Polaroid picture- A token of memory to remember the first meeting of the Reader Society. The meeting came to an end, goodbyes were said, and everyone looked forward to the next meeting.
