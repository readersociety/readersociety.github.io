Reader Society: Origins

By Austin
Jan 11, 2026

Round two of the Reader’s Society meetup— Oh boy, some things never do get old.
It was a sunny afternoon, where, already, a few early birds were present as the sweet, sweet aroma of ramen & tteok-bokki filled the room. The room was a tad quiet, where strums of a guitar took place along with bits of small talk here and there, but later on that would change once more members arrived one-by-one.

While we waited for the remaining members, we decided to play Mafia. That was the moment the silence finally shattered, and just like that, chaos entered the room. Soon enough, everyone around the table got invested in the game, and everyone wanted to be involved.

At last, the club was complete—everyone had arrived, and it was finally meeting time. The food was also too tempting to ignore, so our book discussion happened in-between words & bites. Questions were asked, opinions about the Berrybrook books were given, and a LOT of tea was spilled. With that, a new book was assigned to us by a fellow member, Claudia, for the month; “What things mean” by Sophia N. Lee. (EDIT: It was changed a while later due to lack of online copies)

After the discussion, we played multiple rounds of Dixit— which everyone also obviously enjoyed judging by the laughter that filled the room, followed by even more Mafia, this time with the whole group present.
Finally, the meeting slowly came to an end. Of course, a majority of us stayed longer than we were supposed to, still enjoying the snacks and the company we brought to one another— But time just moved too fast. We said our goodbyes and made our way home, already looking forward to the next meeting. ​
