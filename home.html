CURRENTLY READING

Skip and Loafer: Volume 1

by Misaki Takamatsu

★★★★★ 5.0 · Society favorite

Mitsumi Iwakura has a ten-year plan: move from her quiet country town to Tokyo, attend a top university, enter politics, and save depopulated rural regions.She has the top-tier grades and the absolute drive to make it happen. But on her very first day at a prestigious Tokyo high school, her carefully plotted life plan hits a massive, chaotic roadblock. Between the bewildering maze of the commuter subway, a dizzying rush-hour crowd, and a profoundly awkward first impression at the entrance ceremony, Mitsumi realizes she is completely unequipped for big-city social norms.

Enter Sousuke Shima: a handsome, effortlessly relaxed, and naturally charming classmate who happens to be running late right alongside her. Despite being total opposites—one intensely over-prepared and earnest, the other easygoing and detached—the two strike up an instant, heartwarming bond. As Mitsumi stumbles through new friendships, karaoke invites, and colorful classmates, her unapologetic sincerity begins to touch the lives of everyone around her.

GENRE YA · GRAPHIC NOVEL · REALISTIC FICTION · HIGH SCHOOL FICTION
READ TIME JUL. 18 - AUG. 23
